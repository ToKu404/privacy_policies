# Privacy Policy

## Introduction

Welcome to **Horse-Hub**! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our platform.

Horse-Hub is a platform designed to help users manage horse-related data such as horse profiles, health records, ownership information, and stable management.

By using our services, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using our services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our services, including:

- **Personal Information**: Name, email address, phone number, and account details.
- **Horse Data**: Information you provide about horses, such as name, breed, age, medical records, training history, and ownership information.
- **Usage Data**: Information about how you interact with the Horse-Hub platform, including accessed features and activity history.
- **Device Information**: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
- **Location Data**: With your permission, we may collect location information to improve platform functionality and services.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- Provide, operate, and maintain the Horse-Hub platform.
- Store and manage horse-related data.
- Improve system functionality and user experience.
- Provide customer support and technical assistance.
- Maintain platform security and prevent unauthorized access.
- Send important notifications such as account updates or system announcements.
- Conduct research and analytics to improve our services.
- Develop new features for horse data management.

---

## 3. Data Protection

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption of sensitive data.
- Secure server infrastructure and database protection.
- Regular system monitoring and security updates.
- Restricted access to personal and horse data by authorized personnel only.

While we strive to protect your information, no system can guarantee absolute security.

---

## 4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. However, we may share information in the following circumstances:

- **Service Providers**: Trusted third-party services that help operate our platform, such as cloud hosting, analytics, and technical support providers.
- **Legal Compliance**: When required by law, regulation, legal process, or government request.
- **Authorized Users**: If you choose to share horse data with other users such as trainers, veterinarians, or stable managers.

---

## 5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Horse-Hub may use cookies and similar technologies to improve the platform, including:

- **Session Cookies** to maintain login sessions.
- **Analytics Cookies** to understand user interaction with the platform.
- **Functional Cookies** to enhance user experience and system performance.

Users may control cookies through their browser settings.

---

## 6. Third-Party Services

Horse-Hub may integrate with third-party services such as cloud infrastructure, analytics platforms, or other supporting services. These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage users to review them.

---

## 7. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- Access, update, or delete your personal information.
- Manage or update horse data stored in your account.
- Request deletion of your account and related data.
- Opt out of certain communications.
- Request a copy of your personal data where applicable.

Requests related to personal data may be submitted through our contact information below.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Horse-Hub is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that such data has been collected, we will delete it promptly.

---

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on our platform.

Your continued use of Horse-Hub after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
