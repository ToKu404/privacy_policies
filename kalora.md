# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: October 7, 2026**

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Kalora** ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the **Kalora** application ("Service").

Kalora is an application designed to support the management, collection, and processing of **organic waste**, including waste pickup scheduling, waste collection management, user and account management, and related operational activities.

By using the Kalora Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

We collect and use your Personal Information only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing, maintaining, improving, and securing the Service.

## Information Collection and Use

To provide and improve our Service, we may collect certain information that can identify or be associated with you, including but not limited to:

* Full name
* Email address
* Phone number
* Account information
* Address or pickup location
* Location information, when required for waste pickup services
* Information related to waste pickup requests and schedules
* Photos or other images submitted through the application
* Transaction or service-related information
* Device and application information
* Other information that you voluntarily provide through the Service

The information we collect is used for purposes including:

* Creating and managing your Kalora account;
* Processing and managing organic waste pickup requests;
* Determining and managing pickup locations;
* Coordinating waste collection activities between customers, couriers, and administrators;
* Recording and managing waste collection and processing activities;
* Communicating with you regarding your requests, schedules, or service updates;
* Improving the functionality, performance, and reliability of the Service;
* Preventing fraud, abuse, unauthorized access, and other security issues; and
* Complying with applicable laws and regulations.

We will not use your Personal Information for purposes unrelated to the operation of the Service without your consent, unless otherwise permitted or required by applicable law.

## Location Information

Kalora may collect and use your device's location information when it is necessary to provide certain features of the Service, particularly features related to **waste pickup and collection**.

Location information may be used to:

* Identify or confirm the pickup location;
* Help couriers or collection personnel navigate to the designated location;
* Manage and monitor waste pickup activities; and
* Improve the efficiency and accuracy of the waste collection service.

Location information will only be accessed and used to the extent reasonably necessary to provide the relevant features of the Service.

You may disable location permissions through your device settings. However, disabling location access may prevent certain features of Kalora, particularly location-based pickup services, from functioning properly.

## Photos and Media

Kalora may allow users, couriers, or other authorized personnel to submit photographs or other media related to waste collection and processing activities.

These images may be used to:

* Document waste pickup activities;
* Verify the condition or type of organic waste;
* Support operational and administrative processes;
* Resolve service-related issues or disputes; and
* Improve the quality and reliability of the Service.

We will only use submitted photos and media for legitimate purposes related to the operation and improvement of Kalora.

## Cookies and Similar Technologies

Kalora may use cookies or similar technologies when accessing web-based components of the Service.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to maintain sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, analyze usage, and enhance the security of the Service.

Third-party services used by Kalora may also use cookies or similar technologies in accordance with their own privacy policies.

## Device Information

When you use Kalora, we may automatically collect certain technical information about your device, including, where applicable:

* Device type and model;
* Operating system and version;
* Application version;
* Unique device or installation identifiers;
* IP address;
* Network information; and
* Diagnostic and crash information.

This information may be used to maintain the security and stability of the Service, diagnose technical problems, prevent fraudulent activities, and improve application performance.

Device information collected for these purposes is not intended to identify you personally unless necessary for security, support, or other legitimate purposes.

## Data Retention

We retain your Personal Information only for as long as necessary to provide the Service, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, and protect our legitimate interests.
